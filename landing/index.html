How it works

Quiet during the visit. Clear at the end.

Listen

Live captions from the microphone with doctor and patient labels. Only chart and comprehension badges appear mid-visit — no pop-ups, no alert fatigue.

Check

Chart, concern, checklist and comprehension agents read each turn against a published guideline. A verifier drops any flag without real evidence.

Hand off

One end-of-visit gaps list for the clinician, and a plain-language plan with read-aloud for the patient.

Four gap types

Every flag cites the line that triggered it.

❓

Red-flag question not asked

Checklist items — onset, radiation, sweating, family history — with no matching question.

Never asked whether the pain spreads to the arm or jaw.
💬

Concern left unanswered

A worry the patient voiced that the clinician never addressed.

“Is this my heart?” → next question, no answer.
📋

Contradicts the chart

What the patient says conflicts with the record.

“No heart problems” — chart lists a prior stent.
👂

Comprehension not repaired

The patient did not hear or understand, and no repeat, rephrase or teach-back followed.

Patient answers “Okay.” to the plan, unchecked.
Under the hood

One coordinator. Six specialists.

Each specialist does one small job. A plain, rule-based coordinator decides who runs and when — so nothing is guessed, and nothing reaches the screen without proof.

For patients

Older adults with hearing loss, low health literacy, or too much to remember.

  • Hearing loss. Captions and read-aloud, so nothing depends on catching every word.55% of adults 75+ have disabling hearing loss
  • Low health literacy. Plans written at or below a 6th-grade level, checked on every summary.Only 12% of US adults are proficient (2003 survey)
  • Memory load. A short list of next steps, warning signs, and questions for next time.40–80% of what clinicians say is forgotten right away
  • Caregivers. A spouse or daughter in the room gets the same plain-language plan.
For clinicians

A safety net that stays out of the way.

  • Quiet by design. No live pop-ups or alert fatigue. One end-of-visit list, with a passive badge only for chart contradictions and unrepaired confusion.
  • Four catches. Red-flag questions not asked, worries not answered, chart contradictions, and confusion nobody repaired.
  • Verifiable in seconds. Every flag cites its transcript line or chart entry and dismisses in one tap.
  • Documentation only. Never suggests a diagnosis or treatment. The clinician decides.

The impact

A quiet revolution for patients and healthcare.

System Success & Revolution

  • Scalable Empathy: Bridging the gap between clinical data and patient understanding at scale.
  • Zero-Risk Adoption: Secure, offline-first architecture removes barriers.
  • AI as a Partner: Augments workflows with deterministic, verifiable outputs.

Direct Patient Impact

  • Empowered Understanding: Transforms jargon into actionable, 6th-grade level knowledge.
  • Reduced Anxiety & Errors: Clear instructions lead to better adherence.
  • A Voice That’s Heard: Ensures every concern is captured and addressed.

Clinician & Domain Impact

  • Alleviated Burnout: Offloads translation and documentation burden.
  • Enhanced Trust & Rapport: Fosters stronger patient-provider relationships.
  • Data-Driven Insights: Structured data for future analysis and quality improvement.
